# Original method sources and official reference points

Reference list prepared with the initial skill on 12 November 2026. This release does not include a link-by-link verification record. Recheck current law, service availability, access conditions, and tool documentation when applying them. An older method article is not evidence of a current interface.

Use these sources to understand the actual methodology or rule. The workflow, templates, verdict vocabulary, and confidence defaults in this skill are an editorial synthesis. They do not confer certification, membership, forensic accreditation, or legal clearance.

## Research and verification methods

| Source | Original reference | Use and scope |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Mike Caulfield | [SIFT: The Four Moves](https://hapgood.us/2019/06/19/sift-the-four-moves/) | Rapid source and context triage; not a substitute for a full investigation |
| Digital Inquiry Group | [Teaching Lateral Reading](https://cor.inquirygroup.org/curriculum/collections/teaching-lateral-reading/) | Assess a source through independent external context |
| GIJN | [Planning and Carrying Out an Investigation](https://gijn.org/resource/citizen-investigations-planning-and-carrying-out-an-investigation/) | Frame questions, test hypotheses, and revise the theory when evidence changes |
| CIA Center for the Study of Intelligence | [A Tradecraft Primer](https://www.cia.gov/resources/csi/static/Tradecraft-Primer-apr09.pdf) | Consult the section on analysis of competing hypotheses; use the reasoning method, not intelligence powers or covert techniques |
| Reuters | [Journalistic Standards](https://reutersagency.com/about/standards-values/) | Accuracy, sourcing, corrections, and challenging an initial story premise |
| IFCN | [Code of Principles commitments](https://ifcncodeofprinciples.poynter.org/the-commitments) | Source, methodology, funding, fairness, and corrections standards for participating organizations |
| EFCSN | [European Code of Standards](https://efcsn.com/code-of-standards/) | Article 2 on verification and review; Article 3 on fairness and corrections |
| OHCHR and UC Berkeley Human Rights Center | [Berkeley Protocol on Digital Open Source Investigations, 2022](https://humanrights.berkeley.edu/wp-content/uploads/archive/2024/02/Berkeley-Protocol.pdf) | Consult collection, preservation, verification, reporting, and Annex IV; adapt proportionately to journalism |
| Society of Professional Journalists | [SPJ Code of Ethics](https://www.spj.org/spj-code-of-ethics/) | Public interest, harm, accountability, and source treatment; professional ethics, not binding law |
